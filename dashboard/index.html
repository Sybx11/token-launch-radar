Method

Implied value is this entry's own claim, worked out from the sealed 2026-11-14 snapshot alone:

  1. Sector multiple. For each sector, the median market-cap / TVL of its live-token protocols (each sector's median capped at 1.00 first, so one wild peer cannot set the multiple), shrunk toward the whole-market median (prior weight 5) so a sector with few peers cannot set a wild multiple. Clamped to 0.05–1.00.
  2. Base. TVL × sector multiple.
  3. Growth. × (1 + 0.4 × 7-day TVL change, clamped to −50%..+100%).
  4. Maturity. × (0.70 + age / 1095 days × 0.30); unknown age counts as 365 days.
  5. Chain breadth. × (1 + 0.02 per extra chain, up to 5).

Coverage. The board ranks all 40 watchlist protocols plus every other rankable pre-token protocol in the snapshot — 226 rows, no arbitrary cap. A protocol is pre-token when the snapshot records no market cap, no CoinGecko id and no symbol for it; versioned pools (a name ending in V2, 2.0 and the like) and exchanges are left out.

Farm plan. Six steps per protocol, derived from its own category, chains, TVL and age: a fresh wallet, one capital position, one qualifying action a week, a hold of 30–60 days, claim only from the protocol's own published link, and a written farm log. Risk notes come from the same record: young age, a sharp 7-day TVL fall, single-chain concentration, and the category's own risks.

What this is not. An implied value is not a prediction that a token will be issued, when it will be issued, or what it will trade at. No protocol here has promised a token. Nothing on this page is investment advice.
